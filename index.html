Why I Like Nature

I like nature because it makes me feel calm and happy. I enjoy going outside, seeing trees, taking pictures, and spending time with my family and friends.

Nature is also important because it gives animals a place to live. You can learn more about parks at the National Park Service website .

Places I Like

Forests

Forests are nice places to walk, relax, and enjoy the fresh air.

Waterfalls

Waterfalls are beautiful, and I like listening to the sound of the water.

Mountains

Mountains have beautiful views and are good places for hiking.

My Outdoor Safety Tips

  • Bring water with you.
  • Stay on the trail.
  • Check the weather before going.
  • Do not throw trash on the ground.
  • Be respectful to animals and nature.
